## Direction contract
THESIS: Every price is a shelf label, the thing the shopper already trusts in the aisle; refuses the generic white-card + green-accent grocery app.
OWN-WORLD: Cool shelf-metal ground, graphite shelf-rail band for header and under every horizontal row, paper-white labels with condensed bold numerals (lira big, kuruş raised small), chrome-yellow discount sticker with red numerals, market colour as the label's top band, green only on things you can press.
STORY: The shopper sees today's cheapest labels, trusts them because each carries its read date, and adds to the list in one tap.
FIRST VIEWPORT: graphite rail header with search; list summary; "Bugünün rafı" panel of market labels (discounted count, average cut, read time) plus discount-depth stickers; quick tiles; deals shelf of labels on a rail.
